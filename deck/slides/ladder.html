Earn autonomy, one rung at a time.

Move up only when the guardrails have proven themselves. Self-merge comes last.

1

Paired

2

Gated

You approve the spec and the plan.

3

Parallel

Several agents, each in its own worktree.

4

Background

Agents open pull requests while you’re away.

5

Scheduled

Recurring runs. You read reports and decide.

The future of working: less typing, more supervising. The talk shows a team where agents merge most of their own work, and presents that as the result of groundwork, not a starting point. Version 2 treats self-merge as an optional sixth rung with four entry conditions: recurring review comments have become types, lint rules or tests; every change ships with evidence CI checks; you track reverts and bugs fixed forward and both stay flat as volume rises; and rollback is one step. Until all four hold, a person merges.
