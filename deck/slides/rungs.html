The code map. Never stale.

Prose

Goals and reasons only. Can be skipped.

06

Human review

Taste and one-way doors.

The synthesis. Every piece of knowledge goes on the highest rung that can hold it, and every mistake is fixed at the highest rung that can catch it. This extends the talk’s ranking in three ways: generated knowledge gets its own rung, prose is kept only for intent and reasons, and evals sit beside tests as a runtime check. The /learn command now asks these questions in order.
