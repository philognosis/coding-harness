An honest comparison. The talk is right that prose is the weakest place for knowledge, and version 1 leaned on prose too much. But the talk assumes someone has already decided what to build; goals, specs and decision records still need words. Neither covers designing types and states before the build, and neither covers evals, which a search or chat product needs because lint and types cannot judge a ranking or an answer. Its comment ban is also only half right for agent systems: docstrings on tools and routes are read by the model, so they are interface.
