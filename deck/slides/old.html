Much of the harness is established engineering practice. Architecture decision records, weighted decision matrices, the technology radar, writing the spec and the test before the code, and CI all predate coding agents. They were often skipped because people rarely re-read the documents. An agent reads them at the start of every session, so the discipline finally pays for itself.
