Idea 04 · Feedback beats instruction

Don’t tell it.

Let it check.

An agent that can run a test corrects itself.
“Write good code” does little. A failing test does a lot.

Agents work in a loop: act, observe, adjust. Give the loop something to observe and quality follows. That is why every plan step in the kit names its own verification, why a hook lints after every edit, and why one script, scripts/check.sh, defines “passing” for the agent, the hooks and CI alike.
