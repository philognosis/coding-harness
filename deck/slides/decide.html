The maximum score. Eight weighted criteria, each scored 1 to 5.

A 1 on either top criterion is a veto. Totals within 10% are a tie. You choose; the agent records the decision.

The decision matrix. Before adding a dependency, a service, a new agent or a model change, /decide lists two to four options including “do nothing”, scores each against these weighted criteria, and recommends one. The weights shown are the kit’s defaults and follow the quality order in GOALS.md; change them to match your priorities. One-way doors such as data migrations or public contracts need your explicit approval whatever the score.
